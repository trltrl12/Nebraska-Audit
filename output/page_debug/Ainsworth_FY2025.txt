  for the year ended september 30, 2025
  both of the government-wide financial statements distinguish functions of the city of ainsworth
  that are principally supported by taxes and intergovernmental revenues (governmental activities)
  from other functions that are intended to recover all or a significant portion of their costs through
  user fees and charges (business-type activities). the governmental activities of the city of
  ainsworth include general government, public safety, highways and streets, economic
  development, community improvement, and environment and leisure. the business-type
  activities of the city of ainsworth include the water, sewer, and garbage funds.
  the government-wide financial statements can be found on pages 13 and 14 of this report.
