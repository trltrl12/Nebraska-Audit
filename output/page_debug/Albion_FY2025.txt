=== page 7 | chars=2851 images=0 src=text | class=-

  for the year ended september 30, 2025
  both of the government-wide financial statements distinguish functions of the city of albion
  that are principally supported by taxes and intergovernmental revenues (governmental activities)
  from other functions that are intended to recover all or a significant portion of their costs through
  user fees and charges (business-type activities). the governmental activities of the city of
  albion include general government, public safety, highways and streets, airport, and culture and
  recreation. the business-type activities of the city of albion include the water, sewer, and
  solid waste funds.

  other governmental funds.
