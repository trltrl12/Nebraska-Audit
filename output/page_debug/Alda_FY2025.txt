  responsibilities of management for the financial statements
  management is responsible for the preparation and fair presentation of the financial statements in
  accordance with the modified cash basis of accounting described in note a and for determining that the
  modified cash basis of accounting is an acceptable basis for the preparation of the financial statements in
  the circumstances. management is also responsible for the design, implementation, and maintenance of
  internal control relevant to the preparation and fair presentation of financial statements that are free from
  material misstatement, whether due to fraud or error.
  in preparing the financial statements, management is required to evaluate whether there are conditions or
  events, considered in the aggregate, that raise substantial doubt about the village of alda, nebraska's
  ability to continue as a going concern for twelve months beyond the financial statement date, including
  any currently known information that may raise substantial doubt shortly thereafter.
