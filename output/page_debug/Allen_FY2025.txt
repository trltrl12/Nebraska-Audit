  MANAGEMENT’S DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS
  As management of the Village of Allen, we offer readers of the Village of Allen’s financial
  statements this narrative overview and analysis of the financial activities of the Village of Allen
  for the fiscal year ended September 30, 2025.
  Financial Highlights
  • The assets of the Village of Allen exceeded its liabilities at the close of the most recent
  fiscal year by $831,189 (net position). Of this amount, $638,695 (unrestricted net
  position) may be used to meet the government’s ongoing obligations to citizens and
  creditors.
  • As of the close of the current fiscal year, the Village of Allen’s governmental funds
  reported combined ending net position of $385,521. Approximately 60.0 percent of this
  total amount, $231,295, is unrestricted net position.
  • At the end of the current fiscal year, the unassigned fund balance for the General Fund
  was $221,402, or 174.4 percent of total General Fund expenditures.

  water, sanitary sewer systems, trash, and general administrative services.

  Basis of Accounting
  In the government-wide Statement of Net Position and Statement of Activities, governmental
  and business-type activities are presented using the modified cash basis of accounting. This
  basis recognizes assets, liabilities, net position, revenues, and expenses when they result from
  cash, certificate of deposit, investment and county treasurer cash transactions. This basis is a
  comprehensive basis of accounting other than accounting principles generally accepted in the
  United States of America.
  As a result of the use of this modified cash basis of accounting, certain assets and their related
  revenues (such as accounts receivable and revenue for billed or provided services not yet
  collected) and certain liabilities and their related expenses (such as accounts payable and
  expenses for goods or services received but not yet paid, and accrued expenses and liabilities)
  are not recorded in these financial statements. Under the modified cash basis of accounting,
  proceeds from the issuance of long-term debt are reflected as revenue and payment of long-term
  debt is recognized as an expense. Capital outlay is expensed. Right of use assets and related
  lease liabilities, as defined by GASB 87 and 96, are not reflected in the accompanying modified
  cash basis financial statements.
  4.
