  twelve-month support staff (40 hours per week with a minimum of 260 days per year) is allowed one-week
  vacation after one-year employment and two weeks after five years and three weeks after twenty years with
  pay. unused vacation days will be paid. nine-month full time employees (para educators) are allowed four
  days (accumulate to 15 days) for illness or bereavement and one personal day per year with pay. nine-
  month part time employees (bus drivers) are allowed three days leave. bus drivers with ten years of service
  or more are allowed four days. certified staff receives twelve days of paid leave annually. unused days are
  paid at the rate of % substitute pay per day or they can be banked for up to forty days. unused sick leave is
  not paid.

  7. during our review of activity fund transactions, we noted several expenditures that appear to be
  general fund-type expenses being paid through the activity fund account titled “general activities
  expense.” these expenditures are primarily funded through general fund support transfers, meaning
  they are, in substance, general fund expenditures. the nebraska department of education's
  accounting and reporting manual specifies that the activity fund should be used only for student
  activity-related purposes and that operating or general fund-type expenditures are not allowable from
  the activity fund. paying such expenditures through the activity fund may result in improper
  accounting classification and noncompliance with state guidance. we recommend that management
  review expenditures charged to the activity fund and ensure that all general fund-type expenses are
  paid directly from the general fund. transfers to the activity fund should be limited to true student
  activity purposes, and all accounts within the activity fund should be periodically reviewed to ensure
  compliance with allowable use requirements.
