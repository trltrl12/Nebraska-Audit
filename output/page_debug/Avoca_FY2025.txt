  an answer, you may attach a sheet of paper to the back of this questionnaire. All questions should be answered .
  If Yes, who conducted the bank reconciliation, how were they performed, when was this usually completed and
  was the board in any way involved (such as reviewing or approving)?
  Each month the Village Clerk completes the bank reconciliations for each bank account. The Board approves
  the monthly receipts and disbursements of the Village at each monthly meeting. Balances and activity are
  reviewed by the Board.
  If No, what was the reason the bank reconciliations were not performed? What was done to compensate for
  not doing bank reconciliations, who did this, when was this done, and what was the board's involvement?
  n/a
  If Yes, who maintains the listing, what is the policy of the Village for late payments or no payments, and what is
  the board's involvement?
  The Utility Clerk maintains the listing. The utility program prints out a report of delinquencies and letters are
  automatically printed. If payments are not received, the utility clerk mails these letters by certified mail. Water is
  shut off when bills are not paid and a reconnect fee is assessed.

  MINUTES OF AVOCA VILLAGE BOARD OF TRUSTEES
  SPECIAL MEETING —- BUDGET HEARING
  DATE: Tuesday, September
  30, 2025
  PRESENT: Lehn, Cruz, Kirchhoff, McCay, Nielsen and Clerk - Kirchhoff
  ABSENT:
  OTHERS: Melinda Clarke, CPA and Nina
  The meeting was brought to order at 6:30 pm by Chairman, Chris Lehn. All Board
  members, clerk and two guests were present. Chairman reminded everyone of the Open
  Meetings Act posted on the wall, the attendance was noted by the Clerk and the Pledge of
  Allegiance recited.
  A motion was made by Nielsen to approve the Audit Waiver for 2024-2025 fiscal year. The
  motion was seconded by Cruz. Upona roll call vote of Trustees: Cruz — yes, Kirchhoff— yes, Lehn — yes,
  McCay — yes, Nielsen — yes. Motion approved for Audit Waiver.
  The Budget, prepared by Melinda Clarke, CPA, was submitted to the Board for review. Clarke
  then reviewed the Budget page by page with the Board to explain and identify new information. After
  reviewing, a motion was made by Nielsen to approve the prepared budget. The motion was seconded
  by McCay.
  Upona
  roll call vote of Trustees: Cruz—ves, Kirchhoff —yes, Lehn —yes, McCay — ves.

  VILLAGE OF AVOCA
  SATEMENT OF WAGES & SALARIES
  In compliance with Statutes of the State of Nebraska, Section 19-1102, the following list
  of salaries and wages, contract and otherwise, of the Village of Avoca, as of August 2025, is
  hereby published. Board of Trustees — Chairman $45.00 per meeting if in attendance. Other
  Trustees - $30.00 per meeting if in attendance. Village Clerk/Treasurer - $1020.00, restricted to
  60 hours per month, plus $0.6.67 mileage, plus actual out of pocket expense, plus $17.00 per
  hour for miscellaneous services or services beyond 60 hours. Utility Collections Clerk - $340.00,
  20 hours per month plus $0.67 mileage, plus actual out of pocket expense, plus $17.00 per hour
  of miscellaneous services. Certified Water Operator - $438.86 per month plus $20.00 per hour
  for extra services, plus $0.67 mileage, plus actual out of pocket expense. Certified Waste Water
  Operator - $1245.59 per month, plus $20.00 per hour for extra services, plus $0.67 mileage,
  plus actual out of pocket expenses. Any Miscellaneous labor - $17.00 per hour and $0.67
  mileage as authorized by the Village Board. Utility water shut off - $10.00 and turn on - $10.00.
  Locator - $100 per location of water and sewer lines.
