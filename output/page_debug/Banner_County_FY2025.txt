=== page 18 | chars=2712 images=0 src=text | class=-
  banner county, nebraska
  notes to financial statements
  note 1. summary of significant accounting policies (continued)
  h. property tax
  the county's primary source of revenue is tax levied on the property within the county's
  boundaries. property taxes attach as an enforceable lien-on property as of january 1,
  following the year of levy. taxes are assessed on a calendar year with one-half of the
  tax delinquent as of may 1, and the other one-half delinquent as of september 1.
  property tax revenues are recognized in the period that the county collects them.
