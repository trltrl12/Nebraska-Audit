  NOTE 9 - SERVICE CONTRACT, CONTINUED
  In January 2016, the City entered into a contract with Nebraska Public Power District (NPPD),
  which will serve as the City’s electrical power supplier. The contract will have a term of 20 years,
  |
  and will continue on a year to year basis thereafter. A written notice of 5 years prior must be given
  to terminate the contract at any time after the 15!" year of the contract.
  Under this wholesale
  power contract, the City is subject to a monthly minimum purchase requirement of 65% of the
  i
  peak demand experienced during the peak demand period of June, July, August and September.
  The minimum purchase requirement is adjusted annually based on the most recent demand
  period. In September 2025, the City extended the contract with NPPD through December 31,
  2060.
