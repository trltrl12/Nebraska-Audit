  Restricted - Amounts that can be spent only for specific purposes because of state or federal laws

  Committed - Amounts that can be used only for specific purposes determined by a formal action

  Custodial Credit Risk - for deposits and investments, custodial credit risk
  is the risk that
  in the
  event of the failure of a bank or other counterparty, the School District will not be able to recover
  the value of its deposits or investments or collateral securities in the possession of a third party.
  2.
  Credit Risk - for deposits and investments, credit risk is the risk that a bank or other counterparty
  defaults on its principal and/or interest payment owed to the School District.
  3.

  Significant Deficiencies in Internal Control
  FINDING 2025-001: LACK OF SEGREGATION OF DUTIES
  The District did not have adequate segregation of duties based on our audit procedures. Adequate segregation of duties should
  be in place to ensure internal control over cash receipts, disbursements, and recording of transactions. The District has a limited
  number of accounting personnel. Because of the lack of segregation of duties, the same employees may participate in multiple
  facets of a transaction.
  Recommendation: Management should remain aware of this lack of segregation and continue diligence in oversight and review
  of transactions.
  Views of Responsible Officials: Management is aware of the above finding, but believes the administration and board are
  actively involved to provide additional internal controls.
  The School District also believes that the cost of hiring additional
  employees outweighs any benefit it would receive due to limited resources of the district.
  FINDING 2025-002: FUND BOOKKEEPING
  We noted that the Employee Benefit Fund general ledger activity is not fully included in the District’s accounting software.
  The Nebraska Department of Education requires posting all funds into the District’s bookkeeping records.
  Recommendation: We recommend that the District post the activity into the District’s accounting software as the transactions
  occur to have accurate records.
  Views of Responsible Officials: The District will ensure all transactions are posted within the accounting software.

  MISPOSTINGS TO INTERFUND TRANSFERS
  We noted two interfund transfers that were improperly coded. The interfund transfer from the General Fund to the
  Nutrition fund should be posted to the 5200 program receipt code for the interfund transfer to the Nutrition fund and
  the 8000 program disbursement code for the interfund transfer from the General fund. The second interfund transfer
  was properly coded as an interfund transfer to the Depreciation fund but did not code properly for the interfund transfer
  out from the General fund. The General fund interfund transfer should be coded as a disbursement for where the funds
  would be intended to be used for in the Depreciation fund rather than the 8000 program disbursement code. We
  recommend that the 2024/2025 Users’ Manual provided by the Nebraska Department of Education should be utilized
  for coding interfund receipts and disbursements to the proper accounts.
