  significant deficiencies in internal control
  finding 2025-001: lack of segregation of duties
  the district did not have adequate segregation of duties based on our audit procedures. adequate segregation of duties should
  be in place to ensure internal control over cash receipts, disbursements, and recording of transactions. the district has a limited
  number of accounting personnel. because of the lack of segregation of duties, the same employees may participate in multiple
  facets of a transaction.
  recommendation: management should remain aware of this lack of segregation and continue diligence in oversight and review

  mispostings to interfund transfers
  we noted two interfund transfers that were improperly coded. the interfund transfer from the general fund to the
  nutrition fund should be posted to the 5200 program receipt code for the interfund transfer to the nutrition fund and
  the 8000 program disbursement code for the interfund transfer from the general fund. the second interfund transfer
  was properly coded as an interfund transfer to the depreciation fund but did not code properly for the interfund transfer
  out from the general fund. the general fund interfund transfer should be coded as a disbursement for where the funds
  would be intended to be used for in the depreciation fund rather than the 8000 program disbursement code. we
  recommend that the 2024/2025 users' manual provided by the nebraska department of education should be utilized
  for coding interfund receipts and disbursements to the proper accounts.
