  The Nebraska Auditor of Public Accounts (APA) has reviewed the audit waiver request received from the Village
  of Belvidere (Village) for the fiscal year ending 2025. That request has been approved.
  While performing, pursuant to Neb. Rev. Stat. § 84-304 (Supp. 2025), the preliminary examination necessary to
  determine whether the audit waiver should be allowed or further audit work would be required, the APA noted the
  following compliance matter, within the Village.
  The following information is intended to improve internal controls or result in other operational efficiencies.
  Comment and Recommendation
  Job and Wage Publication
  The APA noted that the Village failed to publish its annual job and wage publication between July 15, 2025, and
  August 15, 2025, as required by State statute. However, the publication was done on October 22, 2025.
  Neb. Rev. Stat. § 19-1102 (Reissue 2022) states, in relevant part, the following:
  Between July 15 and August 15 of each year, the employee job titles and the current annual, monthly, or hourly
  salaries corresponding to such job titles shall be published. Each job title published shall be descriptive and indicative
  of the duties and functions of the position.

  April 10, 2025
  Belvidere, NE
  A special meeting was held on Monday, March 24, 2025 at 8:00a.m. by the Village of Belvidere board
  members. All members were present.
  Linda opened the meeting regarding the water tower. She turned the meeting over to Randy and he said
  that we need to pull the pump and motor to the water tower. There were signs of possible lightening damage as
  there were wires burnt off. We currently are using a 30hp, 3 phase pump and motor with 240 volts. With a 480
  Volt we would use less electricity to run. Wire will be replaced with a #8 wire. Coil will need to be changed
  and Kyle will take care of this. Also switching to a 480 volt the transformers will be changed, Norris said they
  would do this for us. We will turn this into our insurance.
  Tom made a motion to go ahead and make the changes, Carol 2nd, motion carried. Meeting was
  adjourned at 8:30 a.m. Dianne Waldmeier
  The Village Board of Trustees met in regular session on April 8, 2025, at City Hall at 7:00pm. Notice of
  the meeting had been posted for five days in three public places. Trustees present: L. Hudson, T. Priefert, R.
  Waldmeier, D. Waldmeier, and C. Aude. Absent: none.
  The Trustee’s regular meeting was called to order at 7:14pm by Chairperson Hudson. No Guests
  present. Recognized Open Meetings Act.
  Motioned by Priefert, seconded by Aude, to approve the consent agenda including March minutes and
  April claims. All in favor with R. Waldmeier abstaining, the motion carried.
