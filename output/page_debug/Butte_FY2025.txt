  general obligation bonds
  in august 2000, the village issued general obligation water bonds of $584,000 through
  the usda rural utilities service to fund the water system improvements. the terms of the
  bonds require annual payments of $32,039 each october 1 through 2039, which includes
  interest at 4.5%. the bonds are being financed by the water fund, which is an enterprise
  fund. interest paid for the year ended september 30, 2025, was $16,189.
