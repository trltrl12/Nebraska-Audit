  our consideration of internal control was for the limited purpose described in the first paragraph
  of this section and was not designed to identify all deficiencies in internal control that might be
  material weaknesses or significant deficiencies, and therefore, material weaknesses or significant
  deficiencies may exist that were not identified. however, as discussed below, we identified certain
  deficiencies in internal control that we consider to be material weaknesses:
  segregation of duties
  segregation of duties is a basic, key internal control in any accounting system. appropriate
  segregation of duties ensures that errors or irregularities are prevented or detected in a timely
  manner by employees during the normal course of business. at the most basic level, it means
  that no single individual should have control over two or more phases of a transaction or operation.

=== page 50 | chars=3080 images=0 src=text | class=-
  ideally, duties would be separated so that personnel with no involvement or access to ar
  functions would receive the mail directly and prepare a control listing of receipts. additionally,
  cash payments received at the front office should be received by personnel who are not part of
  the daily processing of receipts. charges should be entered by personnel who are independent
  of ar and cash receipt functions.
  management's response
  we will continue to review this area's current process looking for ways where we can improve
  overall internal controls. we will consider whether we can implement changes that result in the
  appropriate cost/benefit mix. we do feel that with our current limited personnel, we will most likely
  still have areas where an ideal level of segregation of duties cannot be achieved.
