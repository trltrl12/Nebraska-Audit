=== page 8 | chars=2311 images=0 src=text | class=-
  norris public power district
  management's discussion and analysis
  in 2025, the district experienced two federally declared disaster events as designated by the federal emergency
  management agency (fema). these events resulted in physical damage to portions of the district's electric
  distribution system and required significant outage and storm response activities, including mutual aid from other
  power districts and contractors, to restore electric service. as a result of the disaster declarations, the district is
  eligible for reimbursement of certain qualifying costs under fema's public assistance program. the district is in
  the process of identifying, documenting, and submitting eligible costs for reimbursement under fema's public
  assistance program.
  fema disaster dr-4868 was declared following a severe winter storm and straight-line winds that caused
  widespread damage across multiple counties in nebraska from march 18 to 19, 2025. this storm affected five of
  the district's six counties with widespread outages and significant damage to the electric infrastructure that
  required the replacement of 890 poles, many miles of distribution line, transformers and related equipment. total
  fema eligible costs are estimated to exceed $3.8 million, of which 75% will be reimbursable to the district.

  organization
  norris public power district (the district) is a public corporation and a political subdivision of the state of
  nebraska providing electric power to approximately 29,000 customers in a six-county area of southeastern
  nebraska consisting of gage, jefferson, lancaster, saline, seward and thayer counties.
  the control of the district and its operations is vested in a board of directors consisting of members elected from
  political subdivisions of the district's chartered territory. the board of directors is authorized to establish customer
  electric rates
