  office that have been outstanding for over a year. we suggest contacting the payee to determine if the check
  is indeed outstanding. if the recipient has not received the check, the original check should be voided and a
  substitute check issued. if the payee cannot be located, the money should be turned over to the state of
  nebraska as unclaimed property.

  that bids are accessible in a timely manner. this would ensure that the county has retained proper
