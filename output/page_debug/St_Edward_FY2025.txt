  both of the government-wide financial statements distinguish functions of the city of st. edward
  that are principally supported by taxes and intergovernmental revenues (governmental activities)
  from other functions that are intended to recover all or a significant portion of their costs through
  user fees and charges (business-type activities). the governmental activities of the city of st.
  edward include general government, public safety, highways and streets, public works, and
  culture and recreation. the business-type activities of the city of st. edward include the water,
  sewer, and trash enterprise funds.
