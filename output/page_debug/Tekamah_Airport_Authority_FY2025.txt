  f.
  property taxes
  property tax revenue is recognized when collected by the county as the airport authority
  agent within the current period. there are no property taxes due which are not delinquent and
  all delinquent taxes are not considered collectible as of july 31, 2025.
  the airport authority's december 31, 2024 valuation was $135,625,426. the levy for
  the airport authority for the year ending july 31, 2025 was .038 per $100 of value.
  the total tax levy was $50,000. property taxes are collected by the county and are
  due december 31 of each year and delinquent in halves at may 1 and september 1 of
  the following year.

  note #3 -- risk management
  the tekamah airport authority is exposed to various risks of loss related to torts; theft, damage to
  and destruction of assets; errors and omissions; injuries to employees; and natural disasters.
  these risks are covered by the purchase of commercial insurance. the airport authority
  assumes liability for any deductibles and claims in excess of coverage limitations. settled claims
  from these risks have not exceeded commercial insurance coverage in any of the past three fiscal
  years.

  note #5 - allowance for doubtful accounts
  the airport authority uses the direct write-off for uncollectible accounts. the doubtful
  accounts at july 31, 2025 were not significant.
  note #6 - concentration of credit risk
  all of the receivables of the tekamah airport authority proprietary funds are from the local
  tekamah area therefore creating a concentration of credit risk.
  if the tekamah area
  economy was depressed, this could have an adverse effect on the collection of the
  outstanding accounts receivable.
