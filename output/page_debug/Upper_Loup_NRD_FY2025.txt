  summary of significant accouting policies
  ‘the upper loup natural resources district, which commenced operations july 1, 1972,
  was formed by an act of the nebraska legislature to replace soil and water conservation
  districts.
  ‘the primary purpose ofthenrd is to carry out various programs in natural resources
  management including ground water management and conservation, tree planting, wildlife
  habitat, soil and water conservation, and information and education. the district provides
  various natural resources conservation services to the counties of thomas, logan, blaine,
  cherry, mcpherson, grant, hooker, and brown.

  cash and investments
  cash includes amounts in demand deposits and certificates of deposit as well as short-term
  investments with a maturity date within three months ofacquisition. the district's policy is

  and intelligence acquire or retain in dealing with the property of another. the district held
