  from material misstatement, whether due to fraud or error, and to issue an auditor's report that includes our
  opinions. reasonable assurance is a high level of assurance but is not absolute assurance and therefore is
  not a guarantee that an audit conducted in accordance with generally accepted auditing standards and
  government auditing standards will always detect a material misstatement when it exists. the risk of not
  detecting a material misstatement resulting from fraud is higher than for one resulting from error, as fraud may
  involve collusion, forgery, intentional omissions, misrepresentations, or the override of internal control.
  misstatements are considered material if there is a substantial likelihood that, individually or in the aggregate,
  they would influence the judgment made by a reasonable user based on the financial statements.

  the village reports the following major governmental funds:
  the general fund is the government's primary operating fund.
  it accounts for all financial

  keno fund and street department. revenue and expenses are maintained separately to
  account for certain state revenue matching requirements and restrictions.

  note #10 -- excess expenditures
  the street, water and sewer funds had excess actual expenditures over the budgeted
  expenditures for the year ended september 30, 2025. the street fund was over budget
  due to street improvements that were budgeted in the prior year and were not paid until the
  current year. the water and sewer funds were over budget due to depreciation and
  unexpected repairs.

  the village reports its revenues on taxable utilities net of sales tax collected.
